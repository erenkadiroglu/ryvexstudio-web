When you publish an app, your name shows up as a small line in the store. A game ends up under another account, a web project at another address. The products may be good, but they don't know about each other.

Ryvex Studio was founded to bring that together. Mobile apps, games and web products are now released under one roof, with one signature.

## What does one signature mean?

Each product answers a different need. FileConvert handles your PDF and file tasks without them ever leaving your phone. PathWords is a puzzle game you play in a short break that teaches English words along the way. PerSayar reads your Okey rack with the camera and counts your sets. But all of them pass the same question: **Would we want to use this ourselves?**

Until the answer is yes, the product doesn't ship.

## What we care about

- **Care.** Details are never an accident. Where a button sits, how long a sentence is, how long a transition takes; each one is a decision.
- **Transparency.** Our products say clearly what they do and what data they use. Every product's privacy policy is one click away.
- **Reachability.** Behind every product is a named founder and a real support channel.
